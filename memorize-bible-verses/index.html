12 well-loved verses (King James Version)

KJV text, which is in the public domain. Long verses: use “Listen and review” mode and check yourself against the verse shown.

  2. Psalm 23:1The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.
  3. Proverbs 3:5Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.
  4. Philippians 4:13I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.
  5. Romans 8:28And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.

Use your own verses and translation

Working through a memory plan with your church, small group or class? Put each reference in one column and the verse in another, then paste or import it. Check your translation’s copyright before sharing a deck publicly.

Two ways to practice

“Answer out loud” listens and checks what you said, which works best for short verses. For long passages, “Listen and review” reads the reference, gives you time to recite, then shows the verse.

FAQ

Questions about this deck

Which Bible translation is this deck?

The King James Version, which is in the public domain. You can make a deck in any translation you have the right to use.

Will it understand me if I recite a long verse?

Speech recognition is less reliable on long passages. Use “Listen and review” mode for those and check yourself against the verse on screen.

Is it free?

Yes. The deck and the core app are free, with no account.

Can my small group use the same deck?

Yes. Share this page, or put your group’s verses in a Google Sheet that everyone loads into same3le.
