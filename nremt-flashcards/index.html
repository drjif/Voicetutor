EMT starter deck

16 cards based on widely taught guidance. Your local protocols and current AHA and NREMT materials always come first.

  1. Adult chest compression rate?100 to 120 per minute
  2. Adult chest compression depth?At least 2 inches, no more than 2.4 inches
  3. Compression-to-breath ratio for adult CPR?30 to 2
  4. Two-rescuer compression-to-breath ratio for a child?15 to 2
  5. Infant chest compression depth?About 1.5 inches
  6. Lowest and highest possible Glasgow Coma Scale scores?3 and 15
  7. What three things does the Glasgow Coma Scale score?Eye opening, verbal response and motor response
  8. In START triage, what does a red tag mean?Immediate
  9. In START triage, what does a yellow tag mean?Delayed
  10. In START triage, what does a green tag mean?Minor, the walking wounded
  11. What does AVPU stand for?Alert, verbal, painful, unresponsive
  12. What does SAMPLE history stand for?Signs and symptoms, allergies, medications, past history, last oral intake, events
  13. What does OPQRST help you assess?Pain: onset, provocation, quality, region and radiation, severity, time
  14. Adult epinephrine auto-injector dose?0.3 milligrams
  15. Pediatric epinephrine auto-injector dose?0.15 milligrams

Practice the way you’ll perform

On a call and in skills testing you say things out loud: the scene, the assessment, the numbers. Spoken recall builds that habit far better than rereading. Use the deck between shifts, at the gym or on the drive in, as long as it doesn’t distract you.

FAQ

Questions about this deck

Are these official NREMT questions?

No. They are original study cards about widely taught EMT content. same3le is not affiliated with the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians, and the NREMT name only describes what the cards help you prepare for.

Do these cards replace my local protocols?

No. Medication doses and procedures follow your medical director, local protocols and current AHA and NREMT materials. Edit any card that differs.

Is the EMT deck free?

Yes. The deck and the core app are free, with no account.

Can I use same3le on a call?

No. It is a study tool. Never use it during patient care.
