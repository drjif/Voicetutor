About same3le

Updated August 31, 2026

same3le is a free browser app that turns your question-and-answer list into a spoken tutor. You supply structured questions and answers. The app reads a question, you answer out loud or listen and review, and it continues through the list.

What it is

  • Voice flashcards and spoken Q&A in a current web browser
  • Paste, Google Sheet, CSV, Excel, or Anki import into the same study engine
  • Answer out loud, listen-and-review, or experimental lock-screen review
  • A free core study experience, with optional Pro planned later

What it is not

  • It does not automatically generate questions from PDFs or unstructured notes
  • It is not a medical device, clinical decision support, or a place for patient information
  • It is not a proctored exam and does not certify competence
  • Speech matching is a study aid, not a certified score

Who operates it

same3le is independently built and operated in the United States. A legal business entity name is not yet finalized and will be published before paid checkout launches.

You can reach us any time at info@same3le.com; see the contact page for what to send and what to keep out of email. For the educational and medical boundary, read the educational and medical disclaimer.
