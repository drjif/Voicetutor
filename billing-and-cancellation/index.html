Billing and Cancellation Policy

Effective when paid subscriptions launch.

Free plan

The Free plan does not require payment information. Creating a Free account does not authorize a future charge and will not automatically convert to Pro.

Pro enrollment

Before a user subscribes, checkout must display the exact price, currency, taxes if known, billing frequency, renewal terms, included features, any trial or promotional period, and how to cancel. The user must affirmatively confirm the recurring subscription.

Automatic renewal

Unless checkout expressly states a fixed nonrenewing term, Pro will renew at the disclosed interval until canceled. Renewal reminders will be sent where required by law or promised at checkout.

Trials and promotional pricing

If a trial or discount automatically becomes paid, the enrollment screen and confirmation email must state the conversion date, recurring price, billing interval, and cancellation deadline. No trial structure has been approved yet.

Cancellation

Subscribers must be able to cancel online through an account or billing portal without contacting sales. Cancellation stops future renewal charges. Unless otherwise disclosed, Pro access continues until the end of the paid billing period.

Refunds

The final refund rule has not been selected. Before launch, this section must state whether refunds are offered, the request window, treatment of partial billing periods, exceptions for duplicate or unauthorized charges, and rights required by applicable law.

Failed payments and plan changes

Checkout and account settings must disclose how failed payments, grace periods, downgrades, upgrades, prorations, taxes, and loss of subscriber features are handled. Subscription status should be updated server-side from authenticated payment-provider events, not from browser claims.

Confirmation records

A subscriber should receive a confirmation containing the selected plan, price, renewal interval, cancellation method, and a link to these policies. same3le should retain the accepted legal versions and transaction references needed for support, accounting, disputes, and legal compliance.
