Subscription safeguards

Paid access should be easy to understand and easy to stop.

Affirmative enrollment

A paid subscription will require an explicit checkout action. No prechecked box will enroll a Free user in Pro.

Clear recurring terms

The total price, billing frequency, renewal behavior, trial end date if applicable, and cancellation method will be shown before purchase.

Online cancellation

Subscribers will be able to manage and cancel through an online account or billing portal. Cancellation stops future renewal charges while access continues through the paid period unless otherwise disclosed.

Pricing FAQ

Before Pro launches

Will Free automatically become paid?

No. The planned model requires a separate affirmative Pro purchase. Creating a free account or using the prototype will not by itself authorize recurring billing.

Will Pro require a credit card for a trial?

No trial structure has been finalized. Any trial requiring payment details would clearly show the conversion date, recurring price, and cancellation process before enrollment.

Where will billing information be stored?

Payment-card entry should be handled by a specialized payment processor rather than stored directly by same3le. Supabase is planned for account and subscription-status records, not raw card numbers.

Can I keep using Free after Pro launches?

The current product plan is to retain a meaningful free core experience while offering optional paid account features. Specific plan limits will be stated here before launch.
