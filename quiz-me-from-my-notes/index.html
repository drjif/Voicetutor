A clearer study workflow

From notes to active questions

Notes are useful for organizing information, but rereading them can feel productive even when you cannot recall the material without looking. A question-and-answer format gives you a clear test: can you produce the answer before it appears?

Original note

“The sinoatrial node is the heart’s normal pacemaker.”

→
Question + answer

Question: What is the heart’s normal pacemaker?
Answer: The sinoatrial node.

Three ways to study

Choose how much the app does for you.

Answer out loud

same3le reads the question, listens to your spoken answer, checks for a match, and gives feedback. You can mark yourself correct when the browser mishears you.

Listen and review

Hear the question, use the thinking delay, then hear the answer. This is useful when speaking is inconvenient.

Continuous review

The experimental lock-screen mode combines the remaining prompts into one continuous spoken review. It does not listen or grade.

What same3le does today

  • Reads a structured question list from Google Sheets or CSV
  • Speaks each question and stored answer
  • Listens to answers in supported browsers
  • Uses local text matching to estimate whether an answer matches
  • Saves basic settings and progress in your browser

What it does not claim to do

  • It does not automatically understand every PDF or lecture recording
  • It does not guarantee that generated or imported study content is correct
  • It is not a proctored examination system
  • It is not medical advice or clinical decision support
  • It does not give permission to copy paid or protected question banks

Write better questions

Simple rules for a useful question list

Ask one thing at a time

Short, focused questions are easier to answer and grade than prompts that contain five separate tasks.

Use a complete answer

Save the shortest answer that is still accurate. Add common synonyms in the optional accepted-alternatives column.

Verify your material

same3le reads what you provide. Check facts against your course, textbook, guideline, or other authoritative source.

Frequently asked

Questions about studying from notes

Can I paste a paragraph of notes directly into same3le?

The current version expects separate questions and answers. Automatic conversion of unstructured notes is not yet part of the live app.

Can I use the app for free?

Yes. The current core app is free and does not require a credit card. Optional paid features may be introduced in a Pro subscription later.

Can I use notes from a commercial course?

Only when you own the material or have permission to use it. Do not upload copied paid question banks, recalled secure examination items, or confidential course material you are not authorized to process.

Does same3le upload my CSV?

The current prototype reads CSV files locally in the browser. Future account data may be stored through Supabase, but question-bank storage will remain minimized and clearly disclosed before that feature changes.
