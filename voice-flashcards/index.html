More than text on a screen

What are voice flashcards?

Voice flashcards use spoken questions and answers instead of requiring you to stare at a card and tap a button. They are useful when you want to practice recalling information in complete words, study while walking around, or reduce the temptation to reveal an answer too quickly.

Answer out loud

samme3le reads a question, activates the microphone, compares the spoken response with your saved answer, and lets you correct recognition mistakes.

Listen and review

Prefer not to use the microphone? Hear the question, pause to think, then hear the correct answer automatically.

Lock-screen review

Use the experimental continuous spoken track for passive review. Background playback still depends on the phone and browser.

Why students use spoken recall

Ordinary flashcards can become a recognition exercise: the answer feels familiar, so you assume you knew it. Speaking forces you to produce an answer before seeing it. samme3le makes that process easier by handling the prompt, listening step, feedback, and movement to the next question.

The answer matcher is a practical study aid, not a certified grading system. Speech recognition can mishear names, abbreviations, or technical vocabulary, so the app always lets you override the result.

Voice flashcard FAQ

Is samme3le free?

Yes. The current app and core voice-flashcard workflow are free, with no credit card required. A separate optional Pro plan may be offered later.

Does samme3le create questions from a PDF automatically?

Not yet. The current app uses a structured question-and-answer list from Google Sheets or CSV. It does not claim to convert every document automatically.

Does it save my spoken audio?

The current prototype does not intentionally record or store audio. Browser speech recognition may be processed by the browser or operating-system provider.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It runs in a modern browser and can be installed as a Progressive Web App. Speech recognition and locked-screen behavior vary by device.
