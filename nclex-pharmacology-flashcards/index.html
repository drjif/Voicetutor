NCLEX pharmacology starter deck

25 cards. Verify every fact against your program’s drug reference before an exam.

  1. What drug class is furosemide?Loop diuretic
  2. Which electrolyte do you watch most closely with furosemide?Potassium, for hypokalemia
  3. What is the antidote for heparin?Protamine sulfate
  4. What is the antidote for warfarin?Vitamin K
  5. Which lab monitors warfarin therapy?INR
  6. Which lab monitors an unfractionated heparin infusion?aPTT
  7. What reverses an opioid overdose?Naloxone
  8. What reverses a benzodiazepine overdose?Flumazenil
  9. What is the antidote for acetaminophen overdose?Acetylcysteine
  10. What do you check before giving digoxin?Apical pulse for one full minute
  11. Name an early sign of digoxin toxicity.Nausea and vomiting, or yellow-green halos in vision
  12. Drugs ending in -pril belong to which class?ACE inhibitors
  13. What common side effect makes patients stop ACE inhibitors?Dry cough
  14. Drugs ending in -olol belong to which class?Beta blockers
  15. Drugs ending in -sartan belong to which class?Angiotensin receptor blockers
  16. Drugs ending in -statin lower which lipid most?LDL cholesterol
  17. Which statin symptom should a patient report right away?Unexplained muscle pain
  18. Drugs ending in -prazole belong to which class?Proton pump inhibitors
  19. What is the usual therapeutic range for lithium?About 0.6 to 1.2 mEq/L
  20. Which insulin can be given intravenously?Regular insulin
  21. Name a rapid-acting insulin.Insulin lispro
  22. Which diabetes drug is often held around iodinated contrast?Metformin
  23. Infusing vancomycin too fast can cause what reaction?Vancomycin flushing reaction, also called red man syndrome
  24. Name two toxicities of aminoglycosides.Ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity
  25. How must IV potassium never be given?As an IV push

FAQ

Questions about this deck

Are these real NCLEX questions?

No. They are original study cards about widely taught pharmacology facts. same3le is not affiliated with NCSBN, and the NCLEX name is used only to describe what the cards help you study for.

Is this NCLEX pharmacology deck free?

Yes. The deck and the core app are free, with no account. Tap “Study this deck out loud” and the cards load straight into same3le.

Can I add my own drug cards?

Yes. Paste your own Q: and A: lines into the app, import a CSV, Excel file or Anki deck, or convert a Quizlet set you made with the free Quizlet to Anki converter.

Can I use this to make clinical decisions?

No. same3le is an educational study tool. Always follow current drug references, your facility’s policies and your instructors.
