Good medical flashcard design

  • Ask one focused question per row.
  • Use a concise but complete correct answer.
  • Add abbreviations, eponyms, and alternate terminology as accepted answers.
  • Separate “name the diagnosis” from “name the next step” when both matter.
  • Review and update cards when guidelines or evidence change.

Important boundaries

  • Do not enter PHI, patient identifiers, images, notes, or medical records.
  • Do not use same3le during patient care, procedures, emergencies, or clinical decision-making.
  • Do not assume the speech matcher validates medical accuracy.
  • Do not upload proprietary commercial question banks without permission.
  • Verify medical content against current authoritative sources.

Study modes

Match the mode to the setting.

Answer out loud

Best for focused retrieval when your screen is open. Screen Wake Lock can reduce interruptions in supported browsers.

Listen and review

Useful for a quiet mental response, rapid review, or browsers that do not support speech recognition.

Lock-screen review

Experimental continuous audio for passive repetition. It does not listen, grade, or replace active recall.

Medical student FAQ

What to know before using same3le

Is same3le a medical device or clinical decision-support tool?

No. It is an educational study tool. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, recommend patient-specific actions, or support emergency decisions.

Can I upload a de-identified patient case?

The safest rule for this prototype is no patient information at all. Do not upload cases derived from real patients unless your institution and applicable law expressly authorize the workflow and the service has been reviewed for that purpose.

Can I import UWorld, AMBOSS, or another paid bank?

Only when the content owner’s terms and copyright permissions allow it. A personal subscription generally does not automatically grant permission to copy or process an entire commercial question bank in another product.

Is it free for medical students?

Yes. The current core app is free. A future optional Pro subscription may add cross-device history, multiple saved banks, weak-topic review, and other account features.
